Explainable Differential Diagnosis Assistant

Developed for the Data Science for Business exam, ED²A is an explainable differential diagnosis assistant that processes patient demographics and clinical evidence to generate a ranked list of possible conditions and support telemedicine triage.

Predictions are generated by machine-learning models and do not constitute a medical diagnosis. They must always be interpreted and validated by a qualified healthcare professional.

How to Use

  1. Select the clinical evidence reported by the patient.
  2. Review or remove the selected evidence.
  3. Run the analysis when the information is complete.
  4. Results support, but do not replace, medical evaluation.
